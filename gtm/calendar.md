## Attendance decisions and follow-up

1. **10 September:** HR EXPO is the strongest immediate product-demo audience. grit. overlaps the same day; confirm its venue and access before attempting a double-header. HR Week is the Lithuanian alternative.
2. **17 September:** Choose HR Tech for HR buyers or Startup Fair for suite/startup exposure. Both overlap Nordic Business Forum.
3. **Autumn marketing:** Prioritise Best Internet, the two MarTech conferences and LiMA DAY according to target country. Ask organizers for audience profiles and concrete demo/sponsor options.
4. **Accounting:** Focus local outreach planning on RUP.ee and Finnish accounting contacts on Taloushallintopäivä. Establish whether a supplier can present at the December online software day.
5. **18–19 November:** Choose Slush for fundraising/partnerships or LiMA DAY for marketing buyers; both require separate coverage to attend fully.

Before assigning an exhibitor budget, record the package price including tax, application deadline, remaining availability, included passes, demo facilities and expected audience. Published event dates do not establish booth availability.
